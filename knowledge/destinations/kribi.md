Kribi is a seaside resort town in the South Region of Cameroon, on the Atlantic
coast. It is well known for its long white-sand beaches and relaxed resort
atmosphere, making it one of Cameroon's most popular coastal destinations.

Key attraction:
- **Lobé Falls (Chutes de la Lobé)** — a rare coastal waterfall a short distance
  south of Kribi, where the Lobé River cascades directly into the Atlantic
  Ocean. Local pirogue (canoe) trips are commonly available in the area.

Kribi is also associated with fresh seafood and is a gateway to the southern
Atlantic coast.
