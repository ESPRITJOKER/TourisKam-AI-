Foumban, in the West Region of Cameroon, is the historic seat of the Bamoun
(Bamum) kingdom and one of the country's most important centres of traditional
art and culture.

Key attractions:
- **Royal Palace of Foumban** — the palace of the Sultan of the Bamoun, with an
  associated museum holding royal artefacts, thrones, costumes and traditional
  art.
- **Arts and crafts** — Foumban is renowned for bronze casting, wood carving,
  beadwork and other Bamoun crafts, often sold along its craft street.

Foumban is a highlight of cultural and heritage tourism in the West Region.
