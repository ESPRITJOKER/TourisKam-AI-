Limbe is a coastal town in the South-West Region of Cameroon, situated at the
foot of Mount Cameroon on the Atlantic coast. It is known for its volcanic
black-sand beaches.

Key attractions:
- **Limbe Wildlife Centre** — a sanctuary caring for rescued primates and other
  wildlife, with a strong conservation and education focus.
- **Limbe Botanic Garden** — one of the oldest botanic gardens in Africa,
  founded in the colonial era, with tropical plant collections.
- **Black-sand beaches** — formed from volcanic activity of Mount Cameroon.

Limbe is a common base for excursions toward Mount Cameroon and the surrounding
South-West coast.
