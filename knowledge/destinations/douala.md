Douala is the economic capital and largest city of Cameroon, located in the
Littoral Region on the Wouri River estuary. It is the country's main
international gateway, home to Douala International Airport and the largest
seaport in the Central Africa sub-region.

For tourists, Douala is typically an arrival and transit hub, with markets,
riverside areas and access onward to the coast (Limbe, Kribi) and the West
Region. Neighbourhoods such as Bonanjo are known as administrative and business
districts.
