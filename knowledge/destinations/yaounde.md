Yaoundé is the political capital of Cameroon, located in the Centre Region and
built across several hills (often described as "the city of seven hills"). It
hosts national government institutions, universities and cultural sites.

Key attraction:
- **National Museum of Yaoundé** — presents Cameroonian art, history and
  cultural heritage, housed in a former presidential palace building.

Yaoundé is a common base for exploring the Centre Region and for institutional
and cultural tourism.
